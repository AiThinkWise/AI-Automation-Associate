Problem Scoping

Understanding the current state and defining success metrics

  • No overflow handling
  • No voicemail automation
  • Manual call logging
📅

Scheduling System

Google Calendar for appointments. No automated confirmations or reminders.

  • Manual appointment entry
  • No patient communication automation
  • Calendar-only scheduling

3-chair clinic with limited front desk capacity. Staff handles calls while managing in-clinic patients.

  • Multi-tasking challenges
  • No dedicated call center
  • Limited bandwidth for follow-ups

Voice AI Integration

Purpose: Handle missed calls and after-hours inquiries

  • Answer calls when staff unavailable
  • Collect appointment details via conversation
  • Check calendar availability in real-time
  • Create appointments automatically
Why this approach: Reversible, low-risk start. Can begin with overflow/after-hours only before full coverage.
💬

Automated Confirmations

Purpose: Confirm appointments immediately after booking

  • Triggered when appointment created in Calendar
  • WhatsApp message with appointment details
  • Patient can confirm or request reschedule
  • No PHI stored outside clinic systems
Why WhatsApp: High open rates (98%+), familiar to patients, supports two-way communication.
⏰

Smart Reminders

Purpose: Reduce no-shows through timely reminders

  • 24-hour reminder (day before appointment)
  • 2-hour reminder (same-day confirmation)
  • Automated scheduling via n8n
  • Track reminder delivery and responses
Why two reminders: 24h gives time to reschedule, 2h reduces same-day no-shows.
🔒

Privacy-First Design

Principle: No PHI stored outside clinic systems

  • Google Calendar remains source of truth
  • n8n only passes minimal identifiers
  • Analytics track metrics, not patient data
  • All workflows reversible without data loss
Compliance: PHI stays in Google Calendar. Only appointment times and confirmation status tracked in analytics.

06

Risk Mitigation

Proactive approach to potential challenges

Low

Technical Failures

API downtime, workflow errors, integration issues

Mitigation:
  • Error handling in all workflows with retry logic
  • Fallback notifications to admin email
  • Daily monitoring during first week
  • All workflows reversible without data loss
Low

Patient Privacy Concerns

PHI handling, data storage, compliance issues

Mitigation:
  • No PHI stored outside Google Calendar
  • Analytics track metrics only, not patient data
  • WhatsApp messages use approved templates
  • All data flows documented and auditable
Medium

Staff Resistance

Unfamiliarity, fear of change, workflow disruption

Mitigation:
  • 5-minute training session, not overwhelming
  • Start with after-hours only (no disruption)
  • Emphasize it's reversible anytime
  • Show immediate benefits (fewer missed calls)
Low

Low Adoption/Engagement

Patients don't respond to reminders, low WhatsApp usage

Mitigation:
  • WhatsApp has 98%+ open rates (better than SMS)
  • Fallback to SMS if WhatsApp unavailable
  • Track engagement and adjust messaging if needed
  • Even unread reminders reduce no-shows (awareness)
